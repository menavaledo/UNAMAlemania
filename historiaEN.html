Historia

Since its origins in 1921, the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM) has recognized the importance of international academic links with other higher education institutions, international organizations and governments. Throughout a century, UNAM has undergone a process of internationalization that has allowed its positioning in the international arena, while performing substantive functions of national articulation of higher education institutions and contributing to the global society.

In 2015, the most recent stage of articulation and prioritization of institutional objectives in international matters began. Through the creation of the Coordination of International Relations and Affairs (CRAI).

The Coordination of International Relations and Affairs is the office in charge of consolidating UNAM's presence abroad, through the development of cooperation strategies with higher education institutions, organizations and governments. This office has focused on strengthening the international development of the university through the creation of internationalization policies that streamline and facilitate the processes of research, education and academic and cultural collaboration in the international arena. With the inauguration of this coordination of internationalization, the Secretariat for Institutional Development (SDI) strengthened its tasks related to academic innovation and the strengthening of research inside and outside UNAM.

1921

Creation of the Summer School for Foreigners, today Centro de Enseñanza para Extranjeros (CEPE)

1955

Creation of the Office of Cultural Exchange and Scholarships

1977

The General Directorate for Academic Exchange begins its work

2015

Creation of the Coordination of International Relations and Affairs (CRAI)

1944

Starts the university extension program at the San Antonio Vocational School, USA, currently UNAM-San Antonio (Extension School)

1966

Foundation of the Center for Foreign Language Teaching, the General Directorate for Cultural Relations and Exchange, and the General Directorate for Temporary Courses

2009

Creation of the General Directorate for Cooperation and Internationalization (DGECI)

2017

Creation of UNAM-Germany (Center for Mexican Studies) at the Free University of Berlin
