Who are we?

As part of UNAM's internationalization strategy, representative offices have been established to promote and consolidate UNAM's international prestige. They are responsible for linking UNAM's entities and agencies, with IES in the countries where they are located as well as with countries in their area of influence.

We are the office in Germany, (UNAM Germany headquarters) that was gestated during 2017 and began its functions in February 2018 as a guest institution of the Free University of Berlin inside the International House. Its mission and vision is shared by the eleven offices located in eight countries around the world.

Mission

To promote mobility, networking, cooperation and inter and intra-institutional collaboration from an academic and intellectual development approach; to promote language teaching and Spanish certifications; to disseminate Mexican culture and local cultures, for the benefit of both the university community and the Mexican community abroad; and to enhance the presence of UNAM abroad.

Vision

To be the representation of excellence of UNAM that contributes to the international positioning in its geographical area of influence by promoting academic, scientific and cultural activities.

Objectives

The general objective of the UNAM Germany Office is to be the institutional link between UNAM's entities and agencies and key partners (higher education institutions, agencies and research centers) to facilitate long-term institutional-academic cooperation.

In addition, cross-cutting objectives were established that will enable the headquarters' outreach efforts to result in synergy in international cooperation:

  • Promote frontier research with international perspective initiatives.
  • Add human and financial resources for the development of cutting-edge academic activities.
  • Share specialized infrastructure that gives unique value to cooperation.
  • Promote the strengthening of academic careers through international mobility.
  • Position UNAM as a strategic partner in international cooperation.
